Underwater Photogrammetry & Optical Attenuation

Water attenuates light selectively by wavelength. Red spectrum wavelengths (λ ≈ 650–700 nm) are absorbed within the first five meters of water, whereas blue-cyan wavelengths (λ ≈ 450–490 nm) penetrate deepest. Scientific marine imaging necessitates color-correcting strobe systems and pressure-rated optical domes.
