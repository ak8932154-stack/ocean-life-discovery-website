Endangered

Blue Whale

Balaenoptera musculus
  • Class: Mammalia
  • Depth Range: 0 – 500 meters
  • Diet: Krill Filter Feeder
  • Lifespan: 80 – 90 Years

The blue whale is the largest known organism on Earth. A single specimen can weigh upwards of 150 metric tons, expending gargantuan energy filtering Antarctic krill swarms through specialized keratinous baleen plates.

Endangered

Green Sea Turtle

Chelonia mydas
  • Class: Reptilia
  • Depth Range: 0 – 110 meters
  • Diet: Herbivorous (Seagrasses)
  • Lifespan: 60 – 70 Years

Highly migratory marine reptiles occupying circumglobal tropical and subtropical ocean waters. Their systematic cropping of marine seagrass meadows sustains coastal benthic primary productivity.

Least Concern

Clown Anemonefish

Amphiprion ocellaris
  • Class: Actinopterygii
  • Depth Range: 1 – 15 meters
  • Diet: Omnivorous (Algae & Plankton)
  • Lifespan: 6 – 10 Years

Maintains an obligate mutualistic relationship with sea anemones. Coated with protective glycoproteins, this teleost fish provides tactile cleaning and territorial vigilance to its cnidarian host.

Endangered

Giant Oceanic Manta Ray

Mobula birostris
  • Class: Chondrichthyes
  • Depth Range: 0 – 1,000 meters
  • Diet: Planktivorous Filter Feeder
  • Lifespan: 40 – 50 Years

An elasmobranch exhibiting cephalic lobes specialized for channeling zooplankton blooms into its mouth. Possessing high encephalization quotients, manta rays display sophisticated social behaviors.

Least Concern

Giant Pacific Octopus

Enteroctopus dofleini
  • Class: Mollusca (Cephalopoda)
  • Depth Range: 0 – 750 meters
  • Diet: Carnivore (Crabs & Mollusks)
  • Lifespan: 3 – 5 Years

The world's largest cephalopod mollusk. Equipped with three systemic hearts, copper-based hemocyanin blood, and millions of dermal chromatophore cells facilitating instantaneous dynamic camouflage.

Least Concern

Deep Sea Anglerfish

Melanocetus johnsonii
  • Class: Actinopterygii
  • Depth Range: 1,000 – 3,000 meters
  • Diet: Deep Pelagic Piscivore
  • Lifespan: 15 – 20 Years

Adapted to extreme hydrostatic pressures and complete solar absence. Female specimens lure bathypelagic prey using a modified dorsal ray topped with a bioluminescent symbiotic bacterial bulb.

Least Concern

Moon Jellyfish

Aurelia aurita
  • Class: Cnidaria (Scyphozoa)
  • Depth Range: 0 – 200 meters
  • Diet: Carnivore (Planktonic Larvae)
  • Lifespan: 8 – 12 Months

Characterized by a translucent umbrella-shaped bell and four horseshoe-shaped gonads visible through the mesoglea. Propelled via coronal muscle contractions to capture microfauna on stinging nematocysts.

Vulnerable

Great White Shark

Carcharodon carcharias
  • Class: Chondrichthyes
  • Depth Range: 0 – 1,200 meters
  • Diet: Apex Marine Mammals & Fish
  • Lifespan: Up to 70 Years

An apex lamniform predator maintaining counter-current vascular heat exchange systems to elevate body temperature above surrounding ambient waters, optimizing hunting velocity.

Near Threatened

Emperor Penguin

Aptenodytes forsteri
  • Class: Aves (Sphenisciformes)
  • Depth Range: 0 – 535 meters
  • Diet: Piscivore (Krill, Squid, Fish)
  • Lifespan: 15 – 20 Years

Specialized flightless seabird capable of extraordinary apnea dives in sub-zero polar seas. Possesses solid bone density to counteract buoyancy and oxygen-binding myoglobin stores in cardiac muscle.

Critically Endangered

Staghorn Coral

Acropora cervicornis
  • Class: Cnidaria (Anthozoa)
  • Depth Range: 0 – 30 meters
  • Diet: Photosynthetic Symbiosis
  • Lifespan: Centuries (Colonial)

A foundational hermatypic scleractinian coral that builds architectural scaffolding for shallow tropical reefs. Highly susceptible to elevated sea surface temperatures and ocean acidification.
